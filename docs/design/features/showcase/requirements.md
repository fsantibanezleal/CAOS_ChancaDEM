# Requirements: The showcase: the six views of the visualization catalog, the story mode and the App's stage

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, https://doi.org/10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The bar is ADR-0082 (showcase-grade visualization); the views are the rows V1 to V6 of the SDD's `## Visualization catalog`.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| SH-001 | THE App SHALL reach every view of the visualization catalog (V1 to V6) at the placement its row names, in at most six tabs with the Context tab, and each group SHALL open on one stage that takes at least 60 percent of the App route's viewport (80 percent in Focus). | `frontend/src/App.test.tsx`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G6, G18) |
| SH-002 | THE system view (V1) SHALL draw the three-stage crushing circuit with each stage named by its machine, the selected case's stage active, and its feed and product streams labelled with throughput (t/h), F80 and P80 (mm), power (kW) and the closed-side setting (mm) read from the case's manifest and trace; WHEN a stage is selected, THE App SHALL load a case of that stage. | `frontend/src/views/circuit.test.tsx` |
| SH-003 | WHILE motion is allowed, THE system view SHALL animate the flow along its streams at a speed proportional to the case's throughput; WHILE the reader prefers reduced motion, THE flow SHALL stop and show its direction statically. | `frontend/src/views/circuit.test.tsx` |
| SH-004 | THE population-balance view (V5) SHALL compute p_n = (I - C) sum_{k<n} (B C)^k f from the live engine's matrices for the selected case, SHALL equal the closed-form product p to 1e-9 at n equal to the number of size classes, and SHALL show the residual against n; WHEN the pass count changes, THE size-distribution view (V3) SHALL draw the product after n passes on its axes. | `frontend/src/views/passes.test.tsx` |
| SH-005 | THE story SHALL have five steps at `/story/<step>` (measure, inside, solve, compare, operate), each with a numbered marker, a headline of at most 12 words that states a number read from the release's artifacts or computed by the live engine, one subline, its own stage and one docked panel; WHEN a number is missing, THE step SHALL say so instead of showing a placeholder. | `frontend/src/pages/Story.test.tsx`, `frontend/scripts/check_story_mode.mjs`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G21) |
| SH-006 | THE story SHALL offer next, previous, play and pause, every step directly reachable, the arrow keys and space, a deep link per step, and SHALL pause autoplay on any reader input; WHEN the reader exits, THE App SHALL open with the step's case and group. | `frontend/src/pages/Story.test.tsx`, `frontend/scripts/check_story_mode.mjs`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G21) |
| SH-007 | EVERY primary view and story stage SHALL carry at least three callouts anchored to what they describe, each with a name, a value with its unit, its state and its source, none covering another or its subject. | `frontend/src/pages/Story.test.tsx`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G21) |
| SH-008 | WHEN the reader selects a case, a survey, a size class, a pass count or an hour in one view, THE linked views of its catalog row SHALL show the same selection, in both directions. | `frontend/src/views/linked.test.tsx`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G21) |
| SH-009 | THE comparison view (V6) SHALL draw, for throughput and for power, the held-out MAPE of each method of `hp500-loo` and `hp500-crusherbayes` on shared axes with the ten per-survey errors as points, and SHALL mark the method with the lowest held-out MAPE per target as the recommended one with its reason. | `frontend/src/views/compare.test.tsx` |
| SH-010 | THE campaign view (V4) SHALL never assert a wear rate: the drift rate SHALL be the reader's input, and the story's Operate headline SHALL state P80 per millimetre of setting drift from the engine, not hours. | `frontend/src/views/campaign.test.tsx`, `frontend/src/pages/Story.test.tsx` |
