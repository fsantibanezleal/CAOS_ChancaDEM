# Requirements: The showcase: the six views of the visualization catalog, the story mode and the App's stage

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, https://doi.org/10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The bar is ADR-0082 (showcase-grade visualization); the views are the rows V1 to V6 of the SDD's `## Visualization catalog`.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| SH-001 | THE App SHALL reach every view of the visualization catalog (V1 to V6) at the placement its row names, in at most six tabs with the Context tab, and each group SHALL open on one stage that takes at least 60 percent of the App route's viewport (80 percent in Focus). | `frontend/src/App.test.tsx`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G6, G18) |
| SH-002 | THE system view (V1) SHALL draw the three-stage crushing circuit with each stage named by its machine, the selected case's stage active, and its feed and product streams labelled with throughput (t/h), F80 and P80 (mm), power (kW) and the closed-side setting (mm) read from the case's manifest and trace; WHEN a stage is selected, THE App SHALL load a case of that stage. | `frontend/src/views/circuit.test.tsx` |
| SH-003 | WHILE motion is allowed, THE system view SHALL animate the flow along its streams at a speed proportional to the case's throughput; WHILE the reader prefers reduced motion, THE flow SHALL stop and show its direction statically. | `frontend/src/views/circuit.test.tsx` |
| SH-004 | THE population-balance view (V5) SHALL compute p_n = (I - C) sum_{k<n} (B C)^k f from the live engine's matrices for the selected case, SHALL equal the closed-form product p to 1e-9 at n equal to the number of size classes, and SHALL show the residual against n; WHEN the pass count changes, THE size-distribution view (V3) SHALL draw the product after n passes on its axes. | `frontend/src/views/passes.test.tsx` |
| SH-005 | THE story SHALL have five steps at `/story/<step>` (measure, inside, solve, compare, operate), each with a numbered marker, a headline of at most 12 words that states a number read from the release's artifacts or computed by the live engine, one subline, its own stage and one docked panel; WHEN a number is missing, THE step SHALL say so instead of showing a placeholder. | `frontend/src/pages/Story.test.tsx`, `frontend/scripts/check_story_mode.mjs`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G21) |
| SH-006 | THE story SHALL offer next, previous, play and pause, every step directly reachable, the arrow keys and space, a deep link per step, and SHALL pause autoplay on any reader input; WHEN the reader exits, THE App SHALL open with the step's case, group and view (Look inside on the chamber, Solve on the population balance) and, from Operate, the step's P80 target. | `frontend/src/pages/Story.test.tsx`, `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::Look inside lands on the chamber, Solve on the population balance, Operate on the campaign with its target`, `frontend/scripts/check_story_mode.mjs`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G21) |
| SH-007 | EVERY primary view and story stage SHALL carry at least three callouts anchored to what they describe, each with a name, a value with its unit, its state and its source, none covering another or its subject. | `frontend/src/pages/Story.test.tsx`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G21) |
| SH-008 | WHEN the reader selects a case, a survey, a size class, a pass count or an hour in one view, THE linked views of its catalog row SHALL show the same selection, in both directions. | `frontend/src/views/linked.test.tsx`, `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G21) |
| SH-009 | THE comparison view (V6) SHALL draw, for throughput and for power, the held-out MAPE of each method of `hp500-loo` and `hp500-crusherbayes` on shared axes with the ten per-survey errors as points, and SHALL mark the method with the lowest held-out MAPE per target as the recommended one with its reason; it SHALL open with the runner-up selected, draw the paired gap of the runner-up (or of the selected method) to the recommended one with its 95 percent interval read from `hp500-comparison` as a bracket in a colour of its own with its end values and as the third callout, and state in the takeaway, and first in the story's Compare stage note, how sure the ranking is with ten surveys (the interval and the wins); IF `hp500-comparison` is absent, THEN the gap callout and the certainty sentence SHALL give the readiness reason, never a number. | `frontend/src/views/compare.test.tsx`, `frontend/src/views/recheckFindings.test.tsx::the stage note opens with the certainty sentence the App’s takeaway uses, at the opening state`, `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::the third callout is the paired gap of the runner-up with its interval and wins, read from the document`, `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::adversarial: absent comparison: the gap callout and the certainty sentence give the readiness reason, never a number` |
| SH-010 | THE campaign view (V4) SHALL never assert a wear rate: the drift rate SHALL be the reader's input; the App and the story SHALL open the P80 target by one rule, `defaultP80Target` (the case's P80 plus 10 percent); the story's Operate headline SHALL state the drift response drawn on the stage, the target and the millimetres of setting drift that reach it, never hours and never a local slope; and the P80-per-mm callout SHALL be the secant of the drawn curve from its start to the crossing; the margin callout's state SHALL follow the margin m over the drawn range R (the setting's training range above the current setting): alarm WHEN m <= 0, warning WHEN 0 < m < 0.25 R, normal otherwise, and no state but its reason WHEN the target is not reached in range. | `frontend/src/views/campaign.test.tsx`, `frontend/src/pages/Story.test.tsx`, `frontend/src/views/recheckFindings.test.tsx::the state at both edges: alarm at 0 and below, warning just above 0 and just under the share, normal at the share`, `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::the headline’s two numbers are the stage’s target and its crossing, in both languages`, `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::the P80-per-mm callout is the drawn curve’s secant from its start to the crossing, within 1e-6` |
| SH-011 | THE story's Compare headline SHALL name the recommended method's held-out error as a mean ("on average", "en promedio"), never as a bound. | `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::adversarial: en says "on average", es "en promedio", and the number is the recommended MAPE` |
| SH-012 | WHERE a real survey has a measured F80, THE Measure step and the case tables SHALL show it as measured, and every view that shows the engine's feed F80 (V3, the Look inside panel) SHALL label it as the model's, reconstructed from the measured feed, in both languages. | `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::adversarial: the gradation (V3) labels the engine’s F80 as the model’s, reconstructed from the measured one`, `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::the Measure step shows the survey’s F80 as measured` |
| SH-013 | EACH story step SHALL open on its headline's evidence: Measure SHALL draw the ten surveys' feed rates on its stage linked to the survey scrubber, and Solve and the population-balance view (V5) SHALL open at the pass the Solve headline names (the first whose residual is below 1e-6). | `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::adversarial: Measure draws the ten surveys’ feed rates, 813 to 1,639 t/h at the ends of its strip`, `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::adversarial: Solve and V5 open at the pass the headline names (6 for HP500-01), not at pass 1 or 28`, `frontend/src/views/passes.test.tsx` |
| SH-014 | THE Solve readouts SHALL write one pass in the singular, SHALL show beside the residual the mass sent back at the pass (a quantity distinct from the residual), and SHALL show a residual below 1e-15 as below machine precision, never as a round-off value. | `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::m1: one pass is singular, in both languages`, `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::adversarial: m9: the second quantity beside the residual is the mass sent back over the passes so far, a different number`, `frontend/src/pages/reviewNumbers.test.tsx::adversarial: m11: a round-off residual is shown below machine precision, never as a value` |
| SH-015 | EVERY chart callout of V3, V4, V5, V6 and the Solve, Compare and Operate steps SHALL anchor its leader on the data point as uPlot rendered it (the chart's rendered x scale and fixed y range, its plot area measured against the callout layer at the same instant, re-measured after every render, on a resize of the host, the layer or the plot, and on a scale change), within 4 px of the drawn point at 390 and 1280 px, with and without reduced motion (measured on the canvas by `npm run check:story`). | `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::maps a point the way uPlot’s valToPos does, then divides by the layer the shell multiplies back`, `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::adversarial: the rendered x scale (data-view-x) replaces the declared one, and a malformed attribute is ignored`, `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::the anchors are re-measured after every render and on the layer, the plot and the scale (the source says so)` |
| SH-016 | WHEN a story step changes, THE stage SHALL change its subject first (Solve plays its passes from 1 to the headline's pass, Compare grows the methods' dots one by one, Operate draws the drift curve out to the crossing, Measure and Look inside move their drawing in), then highlight the step's subject, then enter the callouts one at a time in reading order, then build the panel and count its numbers, on the shell's runStagedTransition and flyCamera; WHILE the reader prefers reduced motion, THE first frame SHALL be the final one; a step whose stage is missing SHALL keep its missing state with no animation. | `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::the stages run in reading order: the highlight, then each callout one at a time, then all of them`, `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::adversarial: under reduced motion every stage is applied at once, before the call returns`, `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::the Story stages its steps on the shell’s helpers, never an animation loop of its own` |
| SH-017 | EVERY callout eyebrow and value SHALL be shown whole in the shell's callout card at 390 and 1280 px in English and Spanish (no text cut): the eyebrows and units are short, and a speed, an interval or a machine-precision note is said in the source (measured by `npm run check:story`). | `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::adversarial: the population balance’s units are short in both languages, and its eyebrows are the short ones`, `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::the section’s throw carries its speed in the source, not in the eyebrow` |
| SH-018 | THE chamber section (V2, Look inside) SHALL draw the nip angle between the mantle and the concave at the engine's value, with the grip limit 2 arctan 0.35 as a second, dashed wedge, and SHALL anchor the NIP ANGLE callout inside the wedge, for a surface of revolution and for a jaw. | `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::adversarial: the wedge’s rays meet at the engine’s nip angle and the limit’s at the limit, and the anchor is inside the wedge`, `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::the section of a cone-sec draws the wedge and anchors NIP ANGLE inside it`, `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::the section of a jaw draws the wedge and anchors NIP ANGLE inside it` |
| SH-019 | THE 3D chamber (V2) SHALL name its four parts (mantle, concave, the gap, the feed) on the canvas, SHALL make each selectable by its label and by a click on it (a raycast), linked both ways to the section's parts and callouts on the shell's linked-view store, and SHALL stand on a ground that receives the chamber's soft shadows, its lights and ground the shell's scene environment of the theme. | `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::adversarial: each part of the section selects itself on the store, and a selected part is shown selected with its callout`, `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::a cone-sec: a click on each part's label point picks that part, and the selected part lights up`, `frontend/src/views/showcaseVisuals.test.tsx::a jaw: a click on each part's label point picks that part, and the selected part lights up` |
