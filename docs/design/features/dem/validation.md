# Adversarial validation: The discrete element method on a laboratory jaw crusher, beside the population balance and the measurement

For each requirement of [`requirements.md`](requirements.md), the check that tries to break it: what was planted or refuted, and the adversarial test that plants it. A row is evidence that the requirement can fail, not only that it passes.

The Python tests run from the repository root with `.venv-pipeline/Scripts/python.exe -m pytest <file> -q -p no:cacheprovider`; the tests that run the simulation use a 1 kg batch, two strokes and one overlap limit, never the real bake, and need the package `crushdem` (`requirements-precompute.txt`), which continuous integration does not install: every row that names one also names a test that runs without it. The web tests run from `frontend/` (`npx vitest run <file>`); the layout rules of DM-017 are also measured on the built site by `frontend/src/views/dem.browser.mjs`, run by `npm run check:story` with the story's browser checks.

## Findings of the bake that the checks above recorded

- The 63/52 mm point reproduces the package's committed validation record through the public API with frames on: window mean power 300.8695295854786 W and throughput 2167.063124607173 kg/h (the record's values to the last digit); it completes in 83 strokes, its replay is 996 frames, 839,872 bytes at 12 frames per stroke.
- The 31.5/26.5 mm point does not complete: the overlap error at both limits (the stop is during stroke 6); its document has no window statistic and its verdicts of the particle run say `not compared` with the basis `run_incomplete`; the document's state is `partial` and readiness names the point.
- The 16/13.2 mm point stops at the first limit after 9 strokes and completes at 0.5 in 54 strokes; its replay does not fit the budget at 12 or 6 frames per stroke and is thinned to 4 (1,158,872 bytes).
- The `files` pattern of the case-index schema admitted only `.json` and `.onnx`; the main session decided to widen it (case-index 2.0.0: a changed pattern is a major step by the contract-version gate): the index now lists the three `dem/*.frames.bin` files, read from the document's `simulation.replay.file` in the order of its points and only when the file exists, and `copy-data.mjs` copies them to `public/data/dem/` (the build was run with `CAOS_DATA_ROOT` unset and `frontend/public/data/dem/` held the document and the three frames files). Gates: `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_the_index_lists_a_replay_file_only_when_the_document_names_it_and_it_exists`, `::test_the_case_index_pattern_admits_bin_and_still_refuses_the_rest` (a leading slash, `..`, a backslash and other extensions are still refused), `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_the_replay_file_is_the_one_the_document_names`, `frontend/src/lib/declared.test.ts`.

## Findings of the view that the checks above recorded

- The section of the jaw is a tall, narrow drawing (170 mm across, 560 mm up): drawn alone it filled under a tenth of its stage and failed the gate's painted-area floor, so the legend of the size classes is one drawing with the size mix of the frame beside the section.
- The Spanish page failed the gate on the English source texts of the document (their numbers carry a decimal point); `localizeNumbers` writes them with a comma and leaves DOIs, paths, versions and section numbers alone.
- The three sides' marks at one feed class are drawn a little apart on the size axis so none hides another; the measurement's band for P80 is the reduction ratio's band written on P80 (a product whose F80/P80 is within the band against the measured one lies between the measured P80 over 1 plus the band and over 1 minus the band).
- The mass-held series is drawn in kilograms beside the mass passed (one axis, one unit); the count of spheres held is in the chart's readout and the run's status.
- A run that did not complete printed "+91.9%" on its P80 callout although no verdict rests on it: the model now computes no relative difference for a run whose status is not `complete`, in the callout, the sentences and the takeaway.
- The discharge gap is a few pixels on the section at 1280 by 800: the stage carries a magnified inset of the crushing zone (about 3 times at that size, more on a taller stage), framed, titled as a magnification, with a 10 mm scale bar and the closed and open settings marked; it sits under the size panel, and a stage too short for it (under 90 px of drawing) leaves it out. On a phone it fits under the panel as well. `demModel.test.ts` holds its bounds and scale; the gate holds the stage rules.
- The labels of the section stand outside the chamber with leaders, and the label placer keeps a 22 px margin so the gate's wide-font pass at 390 px cuts none.
