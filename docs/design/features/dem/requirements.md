# Requirements: The discrete element method on a laboratory jaw crusher, beside the population balance and the measurement

Status: planned

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, https://doi.org/10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

The method is the discrete element method with particle-replacement breakage (the Tavares breakage model), run by the published
package `crushdem` on the laboratory jaw crusher of Barrios et al. 2020 (https://doi.org/10.3390/min10080717, CC BY 4.0) at the
three operating points of that paper that have a measured throughput and net power: the feed classes 63/52, 31.5/26.5 and
16/13.2 mm at 6.0 Hz and a closed side of 7.5 mm, batches of 6 kg. The product's own engine predicts the same points with a
laboratory machine, `jaw-lab`. The bands were set before any run of the product (plan of 2026-10-27): throughput within 10
percent, net power within 30 percent, the reduction ratio F80 over P80 within 15 percent. The contract is the catalog row
`dem-run`; the view is the tab `particles` of the App's Validation group. The SDD's `## Visualization catalog` keeps its six
canonical rows: this view adds a second view of two of their purposes (the method at work, and comparison), specified here.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| DM-001 | THE bake command `data-pipeline/run.py dem` SHALL run the laboratory jaw case of the pinned package `crushdem` at the three operating points with one fixed seed and SHALL write `data/derived/dem/jaw-lab.json`, valid against the schema of the catalog row `dem-run`. | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_dem_run_validates` |
| DM-002 | THE product SHALL hold no particle engine, no ore derivation and no run control of its own: the stage SHALL obtain the machine, the ore derived from the paper, the run with its overlap ladder and stall rule, and the window statistics from `crushdem`. | `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_the_stage_takes_the_simulation_from_the_package` |
| DM-003 | IF a run ends without completing (the package's overlap error after its ladder, a stall, the stroke limit or the wall-time limit), THEN THE stage SHALL record the run's state, the stroke at which it stopped and the mass it had passed, SHALL state no window statistic and no verdict that needs one, and THE document's state SHALL be `partial`. | `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_a_run_that_does_not_complete_is_recorded_as_such` |
| DM-004 | THE document SHALL carry, for each operating point: the measurement with its source (the figure and page of the paper, digitised in the package); the particle simulation's steady-window throughput, net power with its standard error, specific energy, product passing at the paper's sieves, d80 and d50; and the prediction of the product's engine at the same point (throughput, power, specific energy, F80, P80, the reduction ratio, the product on the same sieves, its flags). | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_every_point_carries_the_three_sides` |
| DM-005 | THE document SHALL state a verdict per quantity (throughput, net power, reduction ratio) for each pair of sides (particle simulation against measurement, population balance against measurement, population balance against particle simulation): `met` when the relative error is within the band, `not met` otherwise, `not compared` when either value is absent; a verdict that rests on a window SHALL also say whether it is resolved by the package's rule (the nearer band edge more than two standard errors from the window mean). | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_every_verdict_follows_its_band` |
| DM-006 | THE population-balance block of each point SHALL equal the canonical engine evaluated at that point's operating values. | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_the_population_balance_block_is_the_engine_at_the_point` |
| DM-007 | THE operating points, the bands, the laboratory machine's constants, the ore and the run settings SHALL be fixed without reading any measured throughput, power or product of the jaw: WHEN the measured values are replaced by others, THE simulation block and the population-balance block SHALL be unchanged and only the verdicts SHALL move. | `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_no_input_reads_the_measured_outcome` |
| DM-008 | THE document SHALL be a function of the package version, the seed and the committed inputs alone: it SHALL hold no wall time and no date, and a short fixed-seed run SHALL be reproduced bit for bit by a second process. | `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_a_short_run_is_reproduced_bit_for_bit` |
| DM-009 | THE units gate SHALL hold every throughput, power, specific energy and size of the document finite and inside the laboratory machine's physical domain (the domain comes from the machine's nameplate and geometry, never from the measured results), in the bake and again over the committed document. | `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_the_units_gate_refuses_a_value_outside_the_laboratory_domain`, `scripts/check_artifacts.py` |
| DM-010 | WHERE the measured point has a complete run, THE stage SHALL write a replay of the particles (positions and radii of every sphere and the swing plate's position, at a fixed number of frames per stroke over a window of the steady state) to the file the document names, with its byte count and its sha256 digest, recorded without changing the run. | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_the_replay_file_is_the_one_the_document_names`, `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_frames_do_not_change_the_run` |
| DM-011 | THE readiness document SHALL say `missing` for `dem-run` when the document is absent, `stale` when its `engine_version` differs from `VERSION`, `partial` when a run did not complete (naming how many), and `available` otherwise. | `tests/test_readiness.py::test_dem_run_states` |
| DM-012 | THE particle view SHALL draw the particle replay in the jaw's section with the moving plate on its stage, and SHALL carry three callouts read from the document (throughput, net power and product d80 of the particle simulation, each against the measurement, with its unit and its verdict's state). | `frontend/src/views/dem.test.tsx` |
| DM-013 | WHEN the reader scrubs or plays the stroke cursor, THE replay, the per-stroke power and the mass passed SHALL move together; WHILE the reader prefers reduced motion, THE replay SHALL not play by itself and SHALL show the frame at the cursor. | `frontend/src/views/dem.test.tsx` |
| DM-014 | THE view SHALL draw the three operating points on shared axes for throughput and for net power with the measurement, the particle simulation with its standard error and the population balance, the band around the measurement shaded, and SHALL say per quantity which side is nearer the measurement and by how much; it SHALL never present a `not met` verdict as agreement. | `frontend/src/views/demModel.test.ts`, `frontend/src/views/dem.test.tsx` |
| DM-015 | IF `dem-run` is `missing`, `stale` or `partial`, THEN THE view SHALL show the readiness reason in the reader's language and SHALL draw only what the document holds. | `frontend/src/views/dem.test.tsx` |
| DM-016 | THE web SHALL read the document through the generated contract types, and the committed document and its fixture SHALL conform to them. | `frontend/src/lib/contract.test.ts` |
| DM-017 | THE view SHALL pass the shell's measured gate in both themes and both languages at every size the gate walks, with its stage at least 60 percent of the App route's viewport. | `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G6, G13, G19, G20) |
| DM-018 | THE simulation SHALL never run in CI or in the browser: CI SHALL only validate the committed documents. | `scripts/check_ci_budget.py`, `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_the_web_bundle_holds_no_simulation` |
