# Requirements: The discrete element method on a laboratory jaw crusher, beside the population balance and the measurement

Status: planned

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, https://doi.org/10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

The method is the discrete element method with particle-replacement breakage (the Tavares breakage model), run by the published
package `crushdem` on the laboratory jaw crusher of Barrios et al. 2020 (https://doi.org/10.3390/min10080717, CC BY 4.0) at the
three operating points of that paper that have a measured throughput, net power and product: the feed classes 63/52,
31.5/26.5 and 16/13.2 mm at 6.0 Hz and a closed side of 7.5 mm, batches of 6 kg. The product's population-balance engine
predicts the same points with a laboratory preset (`data-pipeline/pipeline/model/crusher/labjaw.py`): the first estimate of
the classification parameters in the literature with their published ranges as a band, the jaw capacity model of Sastri
(1994), and no power, because no work index of the ore is published. Nothing in the preset is fitted to the jaw's measured
throughput, power or product, and no plant machine of the engine changes. The bands were set before any run of the product
(plan of 2026-10-27): throughput within 10 percent, net power within 30 percent, the reduction ratio F80 over P80 within 15
percent. The contract is the catalog row
`dem-run`; the view is the tab `particles` of the App's Validation group. The SDD's `## Visualization catalog` keeps its six
canonical rows: this view adds a second view of two of their purposes (the method at work, and comparison), specified here.

|---|---|---|
| DM-001 | THE bake command `data-pipeline/run.py dem` SHALL run the laboratory jaw case of the pinned package `crushdem` at the three operating points with one fixed seed and SHALL write `data/derived/dem/jaw-lab.json`, valid against the schema of the catalog row `dem-run`. | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_dem_run_validates` |
| DM-002 | THE product SHALL hold no particle engine, no ore derivation and no run control of its own: the stage SHALL obtain the machine, the ore derived from the paper, the run with its overlap ladder and stall rule, and the window statistics from `crushdem`. | `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_the_stage_takes_the_simulation_from_the_package` |
| DM-003 | IF a run ends without completing (the package's overlap error after its ladder, a stall, the stroke limit or the wall-time limit), THEN THE stage SHALL record the run's state, the stroke at which it stopped and the mass it had passed, SHALL state no window statistic and no verdict that needs one, and THE document's state SHALL be `partial`. | `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_a_run_that_does_not_complete_is_recorded_as_such` |
| DM-004 | THE document SHALL carry, for each operating point: the measured throughput, net power and product size distribution, each with its source (the figure and page of the paper, digitised in the package); the particle simulation's steady-window throughput and net power with their standard errors, its specific energy, its product passing at the document's sieves, P80 and P50; and the prediction of the population balance with the laboratory preset at the same point (the capacity model's throughput with its factors, F80, P80, P50, the reduction ratio, the product on the same sieves, the spread over the published ranges of the classification parameters, Bond's energy per unit of work index with the work index that the measured energy implies, and its flags). | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_every_point_carries_the_three_sides` |
| DM-005 | THE document SHALL state a verdict per quantity (throughput, net power, reduction ratio) for each pair of sides (particle simulation against measurement, population balance against measurement, population balance against particle simulation): `met` when the relative error is within the band, `not met` otherwise, `not compared` when either value is absent, with its basis (every power verdict of the population balance, because no work index of the ore is published; every verdict of a run that did not complete); a verdict that rests on a window SHALL also say whether it is resolved by the package's rule (the nearer band edge more than two standard errors from the window mean). | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_every_verdict_follows_its_band` |
| DM-006 | THE population-balance block of each point SHALL equal the laboratory-jaw module of the canonical engine evaluated at that point's operating values. | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_the_population_balance_block_is_the_engine_at_the_point` |

| DM-008 | THE document SHALL be a function of the package version, the seed and the committed inputs alone: it SHALL hold no wall time and no date, and a short fixed-seed run SHALL be reproduced bit for bit by a second process. | `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_a_short_run_is_reproduced_bit_for_bit` |

| DM-010 | WHERE the final attempt of an operating point recorded frames, THE stage SHALL write a replay of its particles (the position and radius of every sphere and the swing plate's bottom edge, at a fixed number of frames per stroke over the attempt, thinned to a byte budget) to the file the document names, with its byte count and its sha256 digest, recorded without changing the run; a run that did not complete is replayed up to its stop. | `tests/contracts/test_dem_run.py::test_the_replay_file_is_the_one_the_document_names`, `tests/test_dem_stage.py::test_frames_do_not_change_the_run` |
