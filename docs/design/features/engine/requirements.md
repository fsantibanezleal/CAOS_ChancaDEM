# Requirements: The crusher engine, canonical in Python and live in TypeScript

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, https://doi.org/10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| EN-001 | THE canonical Python engine SHALL reproduce, for every point of the shared engine reference, every result field of the TypeScript engine within 1e-9 relative, and every flag, regime and envelope code exactly. | `tests/test_crusher_engine.py::test_python_engine_reproduces_the_reference` |
| EN-002 | THE TypeScript engine SHALL reproduce the committed engine reference, so a change to it without a regenerated reference fails the web tests. | `frontend/src/engine/reference.test.ts::adversarial: the TypeScript engine reproduces the committed engine reference` |
| EN-003 | THE live engine SHALL reproduce every baked trace (the summary, both gradations and both sweeps) at the manifest's parameters within the trace's six significant digits. | `frontend/src/engine/crusher.parity.test.ts::adversarial: a one percent change of one product passing value fails the comparison, naming the value` |
| EN-004 | THE Whiten solve SHALL conserve mass and give a product finer than its feed at every valid point. | `tests/test_crusher_engine.py::test_whiten_solve_conserves_mass_and_product_normalizes` |
| EN-005 | WHEN the closed-side setting tightens on one machine, THE engine SHALL give a finer product; WHEN the throw widens at a fixed setting, a coarser product with a higher capacity. | `tests/test_crusher_engine.py::test_tighter_css_gives_a_finer_product`, `tests/test_crusher_engine.py::test_more_throw_widens_oss_and_gives_a_coarser_product_with_higher_capacity` |
| EN-006 | IF the closed-side setting reaches the feed's F80 or its top size, or an input is physically impossible, THEN THE engine SHALL flag the point and never return an unflagged confident result. | `tests/test_crusher_engine.py::test_pass_through_and_invalid_guard_fires_when_css_ge_feed_top_size`, `tests/test_crusher_engine.py::test_envelope_gate_an_implausible_point_is_flagged_and_marked_invalid` |
| EN-007 | THE laboratory-jaw module SHALL compute the capacity of a jaw crusher with the equations of Sastri (1994) as printed (equations 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 to 13), the low-speed form below the critical speed and the high-speed form at or above it, and reproduce the column Vh K1 K3 d of his Table 2 within 0.2 percent for eight of its nine rows and within 3 percent for the fifth. | `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_sastri_table_2_rows`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_otsuka_values_to_four_significant_figures`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_capacity_is_continuous_at_the_critical_speed_and_peaks_there` |
| EN-008 | THE laboratory-jaw module SHALL take as its classification the first estimate of the literature (K1 the closed-side setting, K2 the open-side setting, K3 = 2.3) with K2 never below 1.05 K1, and carry the published ranges of King (2000) as a constant. | `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_the_machine_and_the_first_estimate`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_oss_over_css_lies_inside_kings_alpha2_range`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_alpha2_overrides_the_open_side_and_k2_is_floored` |
| EN-009 | THE laboratory-jaw module SHALL put a narrow feed class (spheres with diameters uniform between its limits) on the engine's grid with its mass conserved, use the class's analytic F80 for the reduction ratio, and refuse limits that are not 0 < d_min < d_max or a class at or above the gape. | `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_class_feed_sums_to_one_and_63_52_lies_in_one_sieve_class`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_class_f80_is_the_analytic_value`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_class_feed_refuses_bad_limits`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_a_feed_class_at_or_above_the_gape_is_refused` |
| EN-010 | WHEN a feed class is evaluated on the laboratory jaw, THE module SHALL give a product through the engine's own breakage and Whiten solve that closes mass, is finer than the feed and gets finer as the setting tightens, a throughput from the capacity model, and Bond's energy per unit of work index with no power; IF the setting reaches the feed's lower limit, THEN it SHALL flag the point as pass-through and still evaluate it. | `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_the_evaluation_closes_mass_and_is_finer_than_the_feed`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_a_tighter_setting_gives_a_finer_product`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_the_result_fields_and_the_analytic_f80_for_the_reduction_ratio`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_bond_energy_per_unit_of_work_index`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_a_setting_at_or_above_the_feeds_lower_limit_is_flagged_as_pass_through` |
| EN-011 | THE laboratory-jaw module SHALL give a sensitivity band over the eight corners of King's ranges, with the lowest and highest P80, P50 and passing at each sieve and the corner that gives each. | `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_the_band_covers_the_eight_corners_and_names_the_corner_of_each_extreme`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_the_first_estimate_lies_inside_the_band` |
| EN-012 | THE laboratory-jaw module SHALL convert fracture-energy parameters to the JKMRC ore competence A*b as 3600 A b' / E50 and say in its documentation that no source states the conversion. | `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_axb_from_fracture_energy` |
| EN-013 | THE laboratory-jaw module SHALL hold no constant that equals or derives from a measured throughput, power or product size of the laboratory jaw it is compared with, and SHALL leave the five plant machines, their presets and the learned models' encodings unchanged. | `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_the_module_source_holds_no_measured_value_of_the_laboratory_jaw`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_the_plant_engine_holds_exactly_the_five_plant_machines`, `tests/test_labjaw.py::test_the_plant_jaw_is_unchanged_by_a_laboratory_evaluation` |
