# Cases

The taxonomy and the coverage matrix of the 27 cases (17 authored circuit cases and the 10 measured HP500 surveys) live in
**[cases/README.md](cases/README.md)**. Each case carries a category, an operating point, numeric expected ranges and a real
or synthetic flag; the App shows one case, Experiments and Benchmark summarise across categories. The cases are indexed
from [frameworks](frameworks.md) by the method they exercise (for instance CP1 and CI1 for the
[regimes](frameworks/14_regimes-and-nip-angle/regimes.md), the ten surveys for the
[HP500 calibration](frameworks/16_hp500-calibration/hp500-calibration.md)).
