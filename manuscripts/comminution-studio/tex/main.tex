\begin{abstract}
\noindent The product size, throughput and power of a cone crusher are set by the closed-side setting, the eccentric
throw and speed, and the feed size distribution. In Chilean copper mining
the concentrator, where crushing and grinding sit, took $55.7\%$ of the sector's electricity in 2024. ChancaDEM is an
open studio that couples a Whiten population balance with an Andersen and Napier-Munn classification, a JKMRC $t_{10}$
breakage function, a reduced Evertsson capacity model, Bond's power law and Morrell's size-specific energy in one engine
in Python and TypeScript that runs in the browser, and holds it against ten measured surveys of a Metso HP500
secondary cone crusher on itabirite iron ore (Rocha et al., 2024). Every fitted quantity is refit without the held-out
survey, beside a constant-mean baseline, a label-shuffle control, a nested $80\%$ band and bootstrap intervals over the
surveys. The calibrated engine reaches a held-out mean absolute percentage error of $\uqQBb\%$ ($95\%$ interval $\uqQBblo$
to $\uqQBbhi$) on throughput and $\uqPBb\%$ ($\uqPBblo$ to $\uqPBbhi$) on power, because its Bond draw is nearly flat
against measured values of $187$ to $355$~kW; a line on the setting reaches $\uqQLine\%$ and $\uqPLine\%$ ($\uqPLinelo$ to
$\uqPLinehi$), and the published throughput model of Rocha et al.\ $\uqQRocha\%$ on throughput. CrusherBayes, the proposed Bayesian calibration
with a Kennedy and O'Hagan structural discrepancy, reaches $\uqQCb\%$ and $\uqPCb\%$ with $80\%$
intervals of coverage $0.80$ and $0.90$, $7\%$ and $44\%$ narrower than the calibrated engine's band; its power
error is not distinguishable from the line's. The surveys contract the noise and the discrepancy slopes
on the setting and on $f_{80}$, the capacity base only through the prior scale of the discrepancy, and not the
classification and breakage scalars. A learned surrogate emulates the engine at $R^2$ from $0.979$ to $0.998$ on an independent draw of $2196$ points.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Comminution, cone crusher, population balance, Whiten model, Bond, Morrell, Bayesian
calibration, structural discrepancy, leave-one-out validation, neural surrogate, mineral processing.
