\begin{abstract}
\noindent The product size, throughput and power of a cone crusher are set by the closed-side setting, the eccentric throw and speed, and the feed size distribution. In Chilean copper mining
the concentrator took $55.7\%$ of the sector's electricity in 2024. ChancaDEM is an open studio that couples a Whiten population balance, an Andersen and Napier-Munn classification, a JKMRC $t_{10}$
breakage function, a reduced Evertsson capacity model, Bond's power law and Morrell's size-specific energy in one engine in Python and TypeScript that runs in the browser, and holds it against ten
measured surveys of a Metso HP500 secondary cone crusher (Rocha et al., 2024), refitting every fitted quantity without the held-out survey and giving bootstrap intervals. The calibrated engine reaches a held-out mean absolute percentage error of $\uqQBb\%$ ($95\%$ interval $\uqQBblo$
to $\uqQBbhi$) on throughput and $\uqPBb\%$ ($\uqPBblo$ to $\uqPBbhi$) on power, because its Bond draw is nearly flat; a line on the setting reaches $\uqQLine\%$ and $\uqPLine\%$ ($\uqPLinelo$ to
$\uqPLinehi$). CrusherBayes, a Bayesian calibration with a Kennedy and O'Hagan structural discrepancy, reaches $\uqQCb\%$ and $\uqPCb\%$ with $80\%$
intervals of coverage $0.80$ and $0.90$; its power error is not distinguishable from the line's. A second evaluation uses a laboratory jaw crusher (Barrios et al., 2020) at three feeds: a discrete element
simulation with particle-replacement breakage and a population balance with a laboratory preset, neither fitted to the measurements, are compared with the measurements. The
simulation completes two of the three batches; its net power is within a $30\%$ band at both ($-\demCoarseErrPsimAbs\%$ and $-\demFineErrPsimAbs\%$), its throughput is $\demCoarseQratio$ and $\demFineQratio$ times the measured one, its product is
coarser, and the third batch stops with the overlap error. The population balance's throughput is $\demCoarsePbQerrAbs$, $\demMidPbQerrAbs$ and $\demFinePbQerrAbs\%$ low, and its reduction ratio is within $15\%$ at one feed.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Comminution, cone crusher, population balance, Whiten model, Bond, Morrell, Bayesian
calibration, structural discrepancy, leave-one-out validation, neural surrogate, discrete element method, particle replacement, jaw crusher, mineral processing.
