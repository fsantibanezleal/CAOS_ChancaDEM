The calibration finds no error in the sampler. Every rank histogram of Figure~\ref{fig:sbc} lies inside the 99\pct{} band
(\sbcOutsideBand{} of the \sbcAllBins{} bins outside it), and Table~\ref{tab:sbc} gives the tests: the smallest of the
\sbcQuantities{} $p$ values is \sbcMinP{}, for \sbcMinPName, and \sbcBelowBonf{} fall below \sbcBonfP, the level that keeps the
chance of any false alarm among the \sbcQuantities{} tests at 5\pct. The ranks of the log likelihood, the test quantity that
detects a posterior close to the prior or one that ignores part of the data~\cite{modrak}, have $p=\sbcLikP$. The first clause of
H6 held. The test has the reach of its size: \sbcN{} posteriors in \sbcBins{} wide bins detect a posterior that is clearly too
narrow, too wide or shifted, the errors that would mislead a band or a mean, and would miss a small distortion in the tails,
which finer bins and several hundred posteriors would be needed to see.

\sbcConverged{} of the \sbcN{} chains met the criterion of Section~\ref{sec:method}, at least 50 integrated autocorrelation
times after the burn-in. The largest autocorrelation time of a chain had a median of \sbcTauMedian{} steps, close to the
\tauTen{} steps of the posterior of the ten surveys, and a maximum of \sbcTauMax: some parameter sets drawn from the prior give
posteriors much harder to sample than that of the real surveys. The \sbcNotConverged{} chains that missed the criterion do not
carry the result. The ranks of the \sbcConverged{} chains that met it, tested on their own, have a smallest $p$ of
\sbcConvMinP{} (\sbcConvMinPName), \sbcConvBelowBonf{} below the Bonferroni level; a subset chosen by a property of the
simulated data is not a calibration test in its own right, so this is a check of the main test, not a replacement for it. No
engine evaluation failed (\sbcRedraws{} parameter sets redrawn).

Table~\ref{tab:verify} tests the second clause. On none of the \verCellOutputs{} cell outputs is the risk of the full posterior
above that of the linearized learned predictor beyond Monte Carlo error: the 95\pct{} interval of the paired difference reaches
zero or lies below it in all \verRiskHeld{}. In \verBelowKO{} of them, power on the ten surveys' design under V0 and under V2,
the sampler does better than the linearized predictor by more than that error, and in \verAtOracle{} of the \verCellOutputs{}
its risk is within Monte Carlo error of the predictor handed the true scalars. The second clause of H6 held, and with it H6.
The comparison is fair to the closed forms: they are given the noise scales, which the sampler learns from the same ten surveys
together with the eight scalars and the six discrepancy coefficients. Where the sampler beats the linearized predictor it does
so despite learning more, so on power it is the linearized model, built at the prior centre, that costs risk, not the
posterior; on throughput the two are equal within error. Within the reach of three cells, this licenses the linearized learned
predictor of the closed-form study and the linearized model of the design study as descriptions of the shipped method. \verConverged{} of the \verPosteriors{} verification chains met
the convergence criterion.

The bands tell a different story from the means. The sampler's 80\pct{} band covered between \verCovMin{} and \verCovMax{} of
the test points across the cell outputs, \verCovPooled{} pooled over the \verPosteriors{} posteriors, against \verCovTrue{} for
the band built with the true scalars and noise scale on the same replicates: a shortfall of \verCovGap{} points, with a 95\pct{}
Monte Carlo interval of $\pm$\verCovGapCI{} points when the two outputs of a posterior count as one observation. The sampler's
bands were \verWidthPct\pct{} as wide as the true ones (95\pct{} interval $\pm$\verWidthCI{} points), and its means carry the slightly larger risk
of Table~\ref{tab:verify}; the study does not separate the two causes. The shortfall does not contradict the calibration.
Simulation-based calibration tests the posterior on average over the prior, with a new truth for every posterior, and a correct
posterior is calibrated in that average; the verification fixes the truth at one point, the posterior mean of the ten surveys,
where a posterior learned from ten surveys need not cover at its nominal rate. Coverage was not part of H6, so the hypothesis
stands, but the number matters to a user: at ten surveys and at this plant the 80\pct{} band of the shipped method is a band of
about \verCovPooled{} coverage.
