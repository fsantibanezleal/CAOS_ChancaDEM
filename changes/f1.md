The surrogate and the autoencoder emulate the UNCALIBRATED engine over per-machine input ranges, so the ten calibrated HP500 surveys (ore A times b 677 against a trained 30 to 110) got nonsense from them (a power of -548 kW) and no alarm. Both model contracts now record those ranges (`training.input_ranges`, written by `stages/train.py` from `sweep.RANGES`, no rounding; the retrained graphs are byte-identical, only the contract JSON changed), and one rule decides in `pipeline.model.bundles.applicability` and `frontend/src/lib/ort.ts`: a model applies when the point is not calibrated and every continuous input is inside its machine's range, inclusive; otherwise the slot's baseline answers (the engine, the envelope gate), labelled `calibrated_case` or `outside_training_range:<input>`. A contract without the ranges fails its schema, so its slot is invalid and the baseline answers. A shared table of 155 points (range edges, just outside, calibrated, NaN, the ten surveys) gives the same verdicts on both sides (the same sha256 is pinned in `tests/contracts/test_models.py` and `frontend/src/lib/ort.test.ts`).
